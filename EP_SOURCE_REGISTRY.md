## Green acceptance gate for the EP

Each of the ten tracks must have:
- one explicitly approved final master;
- final track title and credits;
- final release-art/asset status;
- no unresolved track-version ambiguity.

Until all ten satisfy this gate, the EP remains yellow even if the project-management layer is complete.
