Kfar Blum rules

Every team starts with 5 ⭐, capped at 10 ⭐. Exact song + artist earns ⭐.

Spend ⭐ to replace a song and keep the same turn. Spend ⭐⭐⭐ for a free timeline card.

Revealing the year does not add the card. Only a correct chosen slot enables “Add to timeline”. A played song never returns, even if removed from a timeline.

Turns rotate automatically 1→2→3→4→5→1. The first team to 18 cards wins.

Audio is limited to 30 seconds inside the site. If Safari blocks autoplay, one extra tap on Play completes it.
