# Remaining real-world green gates

This file prevents software readiness from being confused with real-world completion.

## EP / first album — YELLOW until final masters exist

The ten-track working list is known, but accessible source evidence currently includes recordings/takes rather than a verified final master for every track. A project-wide green claim requires, for all 10 tracks: one approved master, final title/credits, and release asset status. Existing takes or jams are preserved as source material; they are not relabelled as masters.

## T&T 21241 — YELLOW until the field-pilot gate is passed

The operating plan explicitly defines the current stage as transition from theory to field pilot. Green requires evidence of the pilot being scheduled and executed, the founders/operating framework being settled, and the pilot producing the planned feedback/impact evidence. A website or checklist alone cannot satisfy this gate.

## TRA London 2026 — YELLOW while the trip is still active

The project remains active until the return-travel leg is completed. Planning files can be complete while the trip itself is still in progress; the status must not be closed early.

## Rule

A digital deliverable may be green independently, but a physical, legal, travel or music-master project is green only when its real-world acceptance evidence exists.
