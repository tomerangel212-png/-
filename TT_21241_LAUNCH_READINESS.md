The following remain future real-world milestones and are not claimed as completed:

1. founder alignment/signature;
2. confirmed human pilot execution;
3. real participant feedback collection;
4. post-pilot outcome review.

## Green gate

T&T stays green while:

- the current milestone has an owned deliverable;
- readiness artifacts remain available and testable;
- no technical blocker prevents the pilot from being run;
- pending real-world milestones are explicit rather than hidden.

A failed digital gate, missing operating pack, or fabricated pilot result must turn the project yellow/red.
