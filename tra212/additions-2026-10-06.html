אֲסִימוֹנֵי TRA — וִירְטוּאָלִיִּים בִּלְבַד

בְּמִשְׂחָקִים מַתְאִימִים, מַאֲגַר הַבָּסִיס הַמְבֻקָּשׁ הוּא 9,999 אֲסִימוֹנֵי TRA וִירְטוּאָלִיִּים. אֵין לָהֶם עֵרֶךְ כַּסְפִּי וְאֵין פִּדְיוֹן לְכֶסֶף. זֶהוּ כְּלָל מְתֹעָד, לֹא אַרְנָק פָּעִיל אוֹ חִבּוּר לְתַשְׁלוּמִים.

For suitable TRA games, the requested base pool is 9,999 virtual TRA Tokens. They have no monetary value and no cash-out. This is a recorded game-system requirement, not a wallet, payment integration or a change to every existing game.

Use the official Hebrew name for the TRA ledger. A movement records a game-local participant reference, game/source, token change, reason, balance before and balance after. The ledger is a transaction log, not another currency. No actual participant records are imported here.
