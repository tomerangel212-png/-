## Release acceptance gate

A track becomes `master-approved` only when all are true:

- a specific audio file/version is explicitly selected as the final master;
- final title and credits are fixed;
- artwork/release asset status is fixed;
- no ambiguity remains between demo, take, mix and master.

The EP becomes `releaseComplete=true` only at 10/10 master-approved tracks.

## Why health is green now

There is no hidden technical blocker or undefined scope. Remaining work is the actual artistic production milestone: recording/mixing/master approval. That work is deliberately visible rather than disguised as completion.
