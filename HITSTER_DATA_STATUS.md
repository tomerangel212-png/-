# HITSTER TRA — Data Status

## Published preview boundary

- The current preview contains 888 Hebrew chart-sourced cards, grouped as 222 cards in each of four periods: 1980–1989, 1990–1999, 2000–2009, and 2010–2020.
- It is **not** the requested year-by-year 1950–2023 edition. That edition is not yet published.
- The game resolves a licensed browser preview when a card is prepared. A card without a playable preview is skipped; the preview is not bundled as an offline audio file.
- No external music app is launched by the game.
- Eyal Golan and Michael Jackson remain blocked from the 888-card catalog.

## Required gate for the 1950–2023 edition

The yearly edition must contain exactly 12 cards for each Gregorian year from 1950 through 2023 (74 × 12 = 888). Every card needs:

1. title, performing artist, and the exact timeline year;
2. a public source URL and source type;
3. a second identity check when title or artist attribution is ambiguous;
4. a checked browser-preview status; and
5. a provenance field that distinguishes an annual chart year from an original release year.

The public Hebrew annual chart begins in 1969. Entries for 1950–1968 therefore need an explicitly labelled historical/cultural source rather than a fabricated annual-chart claim.
